Oral Tolerance Induction for Severe Milk Allergy: The Paper, Explained严重牛奶过敏的口服耐受诱导：论文详解

A beginner-friendly guide to Specific oral tolerance induction in children with very severe cow's milk–induced reactions. Every medical term is defined. Every concept is grounded in analogy.

一份面向初学者的论文导读：Specific oral tolerance induction in children with very severe cow's milk–induced reactions（《对牛奶引起极严重过敏反应儿童的特异性口服耐受诱导》）。每个医学术语都有解释，每个概念都配有生活中的类比。

Paper by Giorgio Longo, Egidio Barbi, Irene Berti, Rosanna Meneghetti, Angela Pittalis, Luca Ronfani and Alessandro Ventura (I.R.C.C.S. "Burlo Garofolo" Children's Hospital, University of Trieste, 2008; J Allergy Clin Immunol 121:343–7) · Explainer published November 22, 2026 · 中文版

论文作者：Giorgio Longo、Egidio Barbi、Irene Berti、Rosanna Meneghetti、Angela Pittalis、Luca Ronfani、Alessandro Ventura（意大利的里雅斯特大学 “Burlo Garofolo” 儿童医院，2008 年；J Allergy Clin Immunol 第 121 卷 343–347 页）· 导读发布于 2026 年 11 月 22 日 · English

Not medical advice. This page explains a research study for learning purposes. The treatment it describes caused frequent allergic reactions, some needing adrenaline (epinephrine), and was carried out under close specialist supervision with hospital admission and 24-hour support. Never try to give an allergic child the food they react to outside such a programme. Talk to an allergy specialist about options.

Read the original paper阅读论文原文

A randomized clinical trial from a children's hospital in Trieste, Italy, published as an Editors' choice article in 2008. It was the first trial to try oral tolerance induction in children with the most severe, life-threatening milk allergy, a group earlier studies had left out.

这是意大利的里雅斯特一家儿童医院开展的随机临床试验，2008 年作为“编辑推荐”文章发表。它首次在牛奶过敏最严重、可能危及生命的儿童中尝试口服耐受诱导，此前的研究都把这类儿童排除在外。

The Big Picture全局概览

Some children are so allergic to cow's milk that a few drops, or even skin contact, can trigger a dangerous reaction. The standard approach was strict avoidance plus an adrenaline auto-injector, which leaves families living in fear because milk hides in so many foods. This team asked whether giving these children tiny, slowly increasing amounts of milk, first in hospital and then at home, could train their bodies to accept it.

有些孩子对牛奶过敏到这种程度：几滴牛奶，甚至只是皮肤接触，就可能引起危险的反应。过去的标准做法是严格回避，再随身携带肾上腺素自动注射笔；但牛奶藏在太多食物里，全家人只能提心吊胆地生活。这支团队想知道：如果先在医院、再在家里，给这些孩子极少量、慢慢增加的牛奶，能否让他们的身体学会接受它？

  1. The most severe cases. Children aged 5–17 with very high milk antibody levels and at least one past reaction needing emergency treatment, a group excluded from earlier studies.最严重的病例。5–17 岁、牛奶抗体水平极高、至少有过一次需要急诊处理的反应的儿童，这类孩子以前的研究都没有纳入。
  2. A fair comparison. 60 children who reacted to very small amounts were randomly split: 30 started treatment, 30 kept avoiding milk for a year.公平的比较。60 名对极少量牛奶就有反应的儿童被随机分成两组：30 人开始治疗，30 人继续避免牛奶一年。
  3. Safety as the main concern. Doses started in hospital with emergency equipment ready, and families had round-the-clock phone support at home.安全是首要考虑。起始剂量在医院给予，急救设备随时待命；回家后，家庭可以全天候打电话求助。
After one year, 11 of 30 treated children (36%) could drink at least 150 mL of milk, about a glass, and 16 more (54%) could take 5–150 mL. None of the 30 children who avoided milk improved. But reactions during treatment were very common, and 3 children had to stop.一年后，治疗组 30 人中有 11 人（36%）能喝下至少 150 毫升牛奶（约一杯），另有 16 人（54%）能耐受 5–150 毫升。坚持回避牛奶的 30 名儿童没有一人好转。但治疗过程中过敏反应非常常见，有 3 名儿童不得不中止。

Background Concepts背景知识

Severe milk allergy严重牛奶过敏

In a food allergy, the immune system makes IgE antibodiesImmunoglobulin E: antibodies that, in allergy, recognise a harmless food protein and trigger the release of histamine and other chemicals. against a harmless food protein, here cow's milk proteins (CMPs)Proteins in milk such as casein, β-lactoglobulin and α-lactalbumin; the parts the immune system reacts to.. When the protein reaches mast cellsImmune cells in skin, gut and airways loaded with histamine; IgE on their surface makes them release it when the allergen binds. coated with IgE, they release histamine, causing hives, vomiting, throat swelling or breathing trouble. The most severe form is anaphylaxisA fast, whole-body allergic reaction that can affect breathing and blood pressure and can be fatal without prompt treatment., treated first with epinephrine (adrenaline)The first-line emergency medicine for anaphylaxis; it opens the airways and supports blood pressure..

发生食物过敏时，免疫系统会针对一种本来无害的食物蛋白产生 IgE 抗体（IgE antibodies）免疫球蛋白 E：在过敏中，它会识别无害的食物蛋白，并触发组胺等化学物质的释放。，这里的蛋白是牛奶蛋白（cow's milk proteins, CMPs）牛奶中的酪蛋白、β-乳球蛋白、α-乳白蛋白等，是免疫系统发生反应的部分。。当这些蛋白接触到表面带有 IgE 的肥大细胞（mast cells）分布在皮肤、肠道和呼吸道中、储存大量组胺的免疫细胞；过敏原与其表面的 IgE 结合时，它们就会释放组胺。，这些细胞就会释放组胺，引起荨麻疹、呕吐、喉咙肿胀或呼吸困难。最严重的形式是严重过敏反应（anaphylaxis）迅速发生的全身性过敏反应，可影响呼吸和血压，若不及时治疗可能致命。，首选急救药物是肾上腺素（epinephrine）严重过敏反应的一线急救药，能扩张气道、维持血压。。

An over-sensitive smoke alarm that goes off at a candle: the alarm itself (IgE on mast cells) is fine, but it has been set to treat something harmless as a fire.就像一个过于灵敏的烟雾报警器，点根蜡烛就响：报警器本身（肥大细胞上的 IgE）没坏，只是被设定成把无害的东西当成了火灾。

Doctors measure milk-specific IgE in the blood in kUA/LKilo-units of allergen-specific antibody per litre of blood, the unit of the CAP test. Very high values (here above 85) predict reactions and make spontaneous recovery unlikely.; every child here was above 85, and the lab test topped out at 100. Reactions are graded 1 to 5 with the Clark and Ewan scaleGrades 1–3 are mild (skin, gut, nose/eyes); grade 4 is moderate (mild throat swelling or mild asthma); grade 5 is severe (marked breathing difficulty, collapse or loss of consciousness)..

医生用 kUA/L每升血液中过敏原特异性抗体的千单位数，是 CAP 检测的单位。数值很高（本研究中高于 85）预示容易过敏，也意味着自然好转的可能性很小。来衡量血液中牛奶特异性 IgE 的水平；本研究每个孩子都高于 85，而检测上限是 100。过敏反应按 Clark 与 Ewan 分级（Clark and Ewan scale）1–3 级为轻度（皮肤、胃肠、鼻眼症状）；4 级为中度（轻度喉头水肿或轻度哮喘）；5 级为重度（明显呼吸困难、虚脱或意识丧失）。分为 1–5 级。

How the trial was set up试验如何设计

This is a randomized controlled trialA study in which participants are assigned to treatment or control by chance, so the groups are alike except for the treatment.. Allergy was first confirmed with a double-blind, placebo-controlled food challenge (DBPCFC)The gold-standard allergy test: the child gets increasing amounts of the food or a look-alike placebo, and neither the family nor the staff giving it know which.. Children were then assigned to groups using sealed envelopesSequentially numbered, opaque, sealed envelopes holding computer-generated group assignments, so no one can choose or predict the next child's group.. The treatment itself was not blinded: families knew which group they were in.

这是一项随机对照试验（randomized controlled trial）靠随机方式把参与者分到治疗组或对照组的研究，这样两组除了治疗之外大体相同。。首先用双盲安慰剂对照食物激发试验（DBPCFC）过敏诊断的“金标准”：给孩子逐渐增量的食物或外观相同的安慰剂，家长和执行的医护人员都不知道给的是哪一种。确认过敏。然后用密封信封（sealed envelopes）按顺序编号、不透明、密封的信封，里面装着电脑生成的分组结果，谁都无法挑选或预测下一个孩子的组别。把孩子分组。治疗本身并不设盲：家庭知道自己在哪一组。

Like a taste test where neither the taster nor the server knows which cup is the real drink: it keeps expectations from colouring the result.就像盲品：品尝者和端杯子的人都不知道哪杯是真的饮料，这样期待就不会影响结果。

Desensitized or tolerant?脱敏还是耐受？

Specific oral tolerance induction (SOTI)Giving a child gradually increasing amounts of the food they are allergic to, by mouth, under medical supervision; today usually called oral immunotherapy (OIT)., today usually called oral immunotherapy (OIT), aims to raise the amount a child can eat without reacting. Researchers distinguish desensitizationBeing able to eat the food only while taking it regularly; protection may fade if daily intake stops., protection that lasts only while the food is eaten regularly, from lasting toleranceProtection that persists even after the food is stopped for a while; often tested as "sustained unresponsiveness".. This one-year study could not tell which one it achieved, and the authors say so.

特异性口服耐受诱导（SOTI）在医生监护下，让孩子经口服用逐渐增加的过敏食物；现在通常称为口服免疫治疗（OIT）。（现在一般叫口服免疫治疗，OIT）的目的是提高孩子不发生反应时能吃下的量。研究者会区分脱敏（desensitization）只有在规律食用期间才能吃这种食物；停止每日摄入后，保护作用可能消失。和长期的耐受（tolerance）停用这种食物一段时间后保护作用依然存在；常以“持续无反应”来检验。：前者的保护只在规律食用时存在。这项为期一年的研究无法判断达到的是哪一种，作者也明确承认了这一点。

Like getting used to cold water by swimming every day: you may lose the adaptation if you stop for months. Whether milk tolerance stays without daily milk is the open question.就像天天冬泳慢慢适应冷水：停几个月可能就不适应了。不每天喝奶，对牛奶的耐受能否保持，仍是个悬而未决的问题。

Choosing the children筛选受试儿童

The challenge began with a drop of milk diluted ten times, doubling every 20 minutes. Only children who reacted by 0.8 mL of whole milk or less were eligible: in 9 of the 60, the threshold was below 0.1 mL. Families with limited access to emergency care, a history of unreliable handling of treatments, or poorly controlled asthma were excluded, because the treatment depends on quick, correct responses to reactions.

激发试验从一滴稀释 10 倍的牛奶开始，每 20 分钟加倍。只有在 0.8 毫升全脂牛奶以内就出现反应的儿童才符合条件：60 人中有 9 人的阈值低于 0.1 毫升。附近急救条件有限、过去处理治疗不可靠，或哮喘控制不佳的家庭被排除在外，因为这种治疗依赖于对过敏反应作出迅速、正确的处理。

Hospital "rush" phase住院“快速”阶段

Group A spent 10 days in hospital. Doses were given several times a day, starting with milk heavily diluted in water and becoming stronger each day until children took undiluted milk, with a planned target of 20 mL. All children took a daily antihistamineA medicine that blocks histamine and dampens mild allergy symptoms like itching and hives; here oxatomide., had an IV line in place, and the team used inhaled (nebulized) epinephrine early for breathing symptoms. Only 9 of 30 reached the 20 mL target in hospital; the rest stayed at lower doses because of frequent reactions.

A 组住院 10 天。每天给药数次，起初是用水大量稀释的牛奶，之后每天浓度提高，直到孩子能喝未经稀释的牛奶，计划目标为 20 毫升。所有孩子每天服用抗组胺药（antihistamine）阻断组胺、减轻瘙痒、荨麻疹等轻度过敏症状的药物；本研究用的是奥沙米特。，留置静脉通路，一旦出现呼吸道症状，医疗团队会及早使用雾化吸入的肾上腺素。30 人中只有 9 人在住院期间达到 20 毫升的目标，其余孩子因频繁过敏反应停留在较低剂量。

Slow phase at home居家缓慢阶段

At home, parents raised the dose very gradually, adjusted to each child's reactions and the family's confidence, following written instructions. They were trained to treat reactions, including with inhaled and self-injected epinephrine, and could call the team 24 hours a day. Children avoided exercise for 3 hours after milk, because exercise can trigger reactions. Once a child could take 150 mL at once, other dairy foods were added. Treatment counted as failed if a child couldn't take at least 5 mL after a year, or had to stop because of reactions.

回家后，家长按照书面说明，根据孩子的反应和家庭的把握程度，非常缓慢地增加剂量。家长接受过处理过敏反应的培训，包括使用雾化吸入和自我注射的肾上腺素，并且可以 24 小时随时联系医疗团队。孩子在喝奶后 3 小时内避免运动，因为运动可能诱发过敏反应。孩子一次能喝下 150 毫升后，再逐步加入其他乳制品。如果一年后仍不能喝下至少 5 毫升，或因过敏反应不得不停止，就算治疗失败。

Like a mountain climber acclimatizing to altitude: climb a little, rest, climb again, always with a guide and a way down if something goes wrong.就像登山者适应高海拔：爬一段、歇一歇、再往上，始终有向导陪同，出现问题时也有下撤的路。

Why 30 per group? (sample size)为什么每组 30 人？（样本量）

The team assumed full success in 35% of treated children versus spontaneous recovery in 5% of untreated ones. To detect that difference with 80% statistical powerThe chance a study detects a real effect of the assumed size; 80% is a common target. at a 5% false-alarm rate, they needed 60 children.

团队假设治疗组完全成功率为 35%，未治疗组自然好转率为 5%。要在 5% 的误报率下以 80% 的统计功效（statistical power）研究能检测出预设大小的真实效果的概率；80% 是常用目标。检测出这个差异，需要 60 名儿童。

Reactions along the way过程中的过敏反应

Reactions were common in every treated child, mostly within minutes to 2 hours of a dose. No child had a grade 5 reaction, and none needed IV fluids, IV epinephrine, resuscitation or intubation. In group B, 6 children (20%) had mild reactions from accidental exposure during the year.

Try it: could chance explain this?动手试：会是巧合吗？

A P valueThe probability of seeing a difference at least this large if the treatment truly had no effect. Small values mean chance is an unlikely explanation. asks: if treatment did nothing, how often would random group assignment alone produce a split this lopsided? This simulation takes the 60 children with their real outcomes, reshuffles them into two random groups of 30 many times, and counts how often one group gets all the successes.

P 值（P value）如果治疗实际上毫无作用，观察到至少这么大差异的概率。数值很小，说明“碰巧”不太可能是原因。问的是：如果治疗毫无作用，仅靠随机分组，多常会出现这么悬殊的结果？下面的模拟把 60 名儿童及其真实结局反复随机分成两组各 30 人，统计有多少次所有成功者都落在同一组。

Limitations局限
  • Small (30 per group), one centre, one year of follow-up; the authors say this can't estimate the true risk of fatal or near-fatal events during treatment.
  • Not blinded, and the groups' outcomes were measured differently (open feeding vs blind challenge).
  • It can't tell whether protection lasts if children stop drinking milk; the authors advise continuing daily milk.
  • The families were highly motivated and carefully selected, with emergency care nearby, so results may not carry over to other settings.
  • The authors note one year is too short to rule out spontaneous recovery in the control group.
  • 规模小（每组 30 人）、单中心、随访一年；作者表示，这不足以估计治疗中致命或接近致命事件的真实风险。
  • 没有设盲，两组结局的测量方式也不同（非盲喂食对比盲法激发）。
  • 无法判断孩子停喝牛奶后保护作用是否持续；作者建议每天继续喝奶。
  • 参与家庭积极性很高、经过严格筛选，附近有急救条件，结果未必适用于其他环境。
  • 作者也指出，一年时间太短，不能排除对照组自然好转的可能。

Why This Paper Matters这篇论文为什么重要

For patients and clinicians对患者和医生

Even partial success changes daily life: a child who can take 5–150 mL is much less likely to react badly to a hidden trace of milk, and parents of treated children reported great satisfaction, even when the gains were partial. The authors stress the cost: frequent reactions, a heavy commitment from family and medical team, and the need for highly controlled settings. They describe it as "no magic bullet".

即使只是部分成功，也会改变日常生活：能耐受 5–150 毫升的孩子，误食隐藏的微量牛奶时发生严重反应的可能性小得多；治疗组家长普遍非常满意，即便效果只是部分的。作者也强调了代价：过敏反应频繁，家庭和医疗团队都要投入大量精力，而且必须在高度可控的环境中进行。他们称之为“没有灵丹妙药”。

For the research community对研究界

This was the first randomized trial of oral tolerance induction in the most severely milk-allergic children, a group earlier studies excluded. Many trials followed. A 2025 meta-analysis of 19 randomized trials (815 children) found that milk oral immunotherapy made desensitization about 2.5 times more likely (RR 2.51, 95% CI 1.54–4.09), with low overall study quality, high variation between studies, and no trial reporting lasting tolerance after stopping. It also found eosinophilic oesophagitis, a gullet inflammation, in about 4% of treated children in cohort studies.

这是首个在牛奶过敏最严重的儿童中进行的口服耐受诱导随机试验，此前的研究都排除了这类儿童。此后又有许多试验跟进。2025 年一项纳入 19 项随机试验（815 名儿童）的荟萃分析发现，牛奶口服免疫治疗使达到脱敏的可能性提高约 2.5 倍（RR 2.51，95% CI 1.54–4.09）；但研究总体质量偏低、各研究差异很大，而且没有一项试验报告停药后的持续耐受。该分析还发现，在队列研究中约 4% 的治疗儿童出现了嗜酸性粒细胞性食管炎（一种食管炎症）。

The bigger picture更宏观的意义

The study marks a shift from "avoid at all costs" toward actively retraining the immune response, an approach now used for several foods. Whether the benefit lasts, and how to balance treatment risks against the risks of accidental exposure, are still being studied. Decisions about it belong with families and allergy specialists.

这项研究标志着一种转变：从“不惜一切代价回避”，转向主动“重新训练”免疫反应，如今这种方法已用于多种食物。效果能否持久，以及如何权衡治疗风险与意外接触的风险，仍在研究之中。是否采用，应由家庭与过敏专科医生共同决定。
